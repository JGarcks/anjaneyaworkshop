## How a volcano occurs (the owner's words are the test)

The owner does not want eruptions scripted, and asked that they be able to occur. Agreed as put to the owner:

> "So no two planets would have the same eruptions, and nobody could say in advance when one is due. The only chance involved is the seed's, as with everything else in Planet, so the same seed still grows the same world.
>
> Each eruption would be logged as an event with its place, size and cause, which is what the brief always meant the history to hold."

The owner: "exactly how I would want it".

In plain words: a rule says when the conditions for an eruption exist, never when an eruption happens. Rock gathers beneath the ground at a rate the planet's own state sets; when enough has gathered it erupts, the store empties, and it begins again.

| Where | What makes one occur |
|---|---|
| Behind a trench | Floor going down fast and deep enough to melt; the faster the closing, the more rock rises (today's arc rock) |
| Over a hot spot | Whatever plate is above it at the time |
| Where a continent is tearing | Crust thinned by stretching |

This is your rule 10 of how we work (nothing on a schedule, nothing steered toward a figure) and rule 9 of the thirteen (one seed, one world). Hot spots set at birth by the seed are a planet born with a past (FS · D4).
